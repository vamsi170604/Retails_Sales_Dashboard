## RetailVision Analytics: Retail Shop Sales Insights Dashboard
An interactive and data-driven Excel dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional business insights through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

# Purpouse of Dashboard
- The Retail Shop Sales Insights Dashboard is a comprehensive Excel project developed to monitor and analyze key retail business metrics such as sales performance, customer segmentation, product demand, shipping trends, and regional profitability.<br>
- The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights that support better decision-making for retailers, business analysts, and sales managers.

# Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
- 📊 Microsoft Excel – Main platform used for dashboard creation and data visualization.<br>
- 📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.<br>
- 📑 Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – Used for summarizing and analyzing large retail datasets.<br>
- 🧠 Excel Formulas & Functions – Used for calculations, KPIs, and dynamic reporting.<br>
- 🗄️ SQL – Used for querying and extracting retail business insights.<br>
- 📈 Data Modeling – Relationships created among customers, products, sales, and regional datasets.<br>
- 📁 File Formats – .xlsx for Excel dashboard files and .png for dashboard previews.

# Data Source
Source: Retail shop sales dataset containing customer transactions, product details, regional sales, shipping information, and order performance data.
The dataset includes:<br>
- Customer information<br>
- Product categories and product details<br>
- Sales and profit metrics<br>
- Shipping and delivery data<br>
- Regional and segment-wise performance<br>
- Discounts and quantity analysis<br>
The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query and Microsoft Excel.

# Highlights of Dashboard
Business Problem :<br>
Retail businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data, making it difficult to track sales performance, customer behavior, and profitability efficiently.
Important business questions such as:<br>
- Which products generate the highest sales?<br>
- Which regions are most profitable?<br>
- How do discounts impact revenue?<br>
- Which customer segments contribute most to business growth?<br>
- What shipping methods are most commonly used?<br>
...are difficult to analyze quickly using spreadsheets or raw datasets.

# Goal of the Dashboard
The primary goals of this dashboard are to:<br>
- Provide a centralized retail sales performance overview.<br>
- Track KPIs related to sales, orders, and profitability.<br>
- Enable interactive filtering for detailed analysis.<br>
- Identify high-performing products and regions.<br>
- Support data-driven business decisions through visual analytics.

# Key Visuals 
KPI Cards
Displays important retail business metrics such as:<br>
- Total Sales<br>
- Total Profit<br>
- Total Orders<br>
- Total Customers<br>
- Profit Margin<br>
- Growth Percentage<br>
- These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance

--> Sales Trend – Year Wise (Line Chart)<br>
This line chart visualizes yearly sales performance from 2019 to 2023.<br>
- It helps businesses analyze:<br>
- Business growth trends<br>
- Year-over-year performance<br>
- Seasonal and market fluctuations<br>
- Revenue growth patterns<br>

--> Revenue & Profit Sales – Category Wise (Clustered Bar Chart)<br>
- This chart compares total sales revenue and profit generated across different product categories such as Daily Products, Electric Appliances, Fast Food, Fruits & Vegetables, Furniture, and Household Items.<br>

--> Quantity Sales – Category Wise (Donut Chart)<br>
- This donut chart displays the percentage contribution of quantity sold by each product category.<br>
- It helps businesses understand customer purchasing behavior and identify products with the highest demand.<br>

--> Regional Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)<br>
This chart compares sales performance across different business regions including:<br>
- East<br>
- North<br>
- South<br>
- West<br>
It helps identify top-performing regional markets and areas with growth opportunities.<br>

--> Profit vs Discount Analysis – Category Wise (Combo Chart)<br>
- This combo chart compares category-wise profit with discount percentages.<br>
- The bars represent profit earned, while the line graph shows discount percentages applied across categories.

# Business Insights
